## The practice

Effective Data Consulting is a data and AI consultancy working with startups and
established enterprises. We take on strategy, architecture, governance, and
implementation work — usually some combination, because the boundary between
advice and delivery is where most consulting engagements fail.

We are deliberately small. Engagements are led by the person doing the work, not
handed to a delivery team after the sale.

## Principal

**Paul Raff** leads the practice. He has worked on data and machine learning
systems at both large enterprises and early-stage companies, which is the reason
the two tracks on this site exist: the constraints are genuinely different, and
advice that ignores that is not worth much.

## How we work

**We say what we think.** If a project is not worth doing, or the thing you asked
for is not the thing you need, we say so early. That is the main reason to hire an
outside party.

**We build, not just advise.** Recommendations are grounded in what is
technically achievable in your environment, because we expect to help implement
them.

**We hand over properly.** Every engagement ends with your team able to operate
what was built. Documentation, runbooks, and working sessions are part of scope,
not an upsell.

**We stay independent.** No vendor relationships, no referral fees, no incentive
to recommend one platform over another.
