The first conversation is an hour, no charge, and no obligation. If we are not
the right fit we will say so, and where we can we will point you somewhere better.

## Email

**[raff@effectivedataconsulting.com](mailto:raff@effectivedataconsulting.com)**

To make the first exchange useful, it helps to include:

- **What you are trying to accomplish**, in business terms rather than technical ones
- **Where you are now** — team size, current stack, what has already been tried
- **What is forcing the timing** — a launch, an audit, a funding round, a migration
- **Your timeline**, even approximately

None of it is required. A single paragraph is a fine place to start.
