Large organisations rarely lack data initiatives. They have several, running in
parallel, competing for the same platform team, with no defensible account of
which should go first.

The constraint here is almost never technical capability. It is sequencing,
ownership, and designing change that can actually clear the review processes it
has to pass through.

## What this usually looks like

- **Competing initiatives, no clear order.** Several credible programmes and no
  shared basis for prioritising among them.
- **Platform consolidation or migration.** Moving off legacy systems without a
  cutover the business cannot absorb.
- **Governance with real obligations attached.** Privacy, audit, and regulatory
  requirements that need an operating model rather than a policy document.
- **AI initiatives stalling before production.** Models that work in a notebook
  and cannot clear risk review, monitoring requirements, or model documentation.

## How we work inside large organisations

**Within your standards, not around them.** Security review, change control, and
platform standards are constraints we design for from the start, not obstacles
discovered at the end.

**Independent of vendors.** No reseller relationships and no referral fees, which
means platform recommendations carry no commercial interest.

**Documented to the standard your processes require.** Architecture decision
records, model documentation, and evidence trails are part of scope.

**Built to hand over.** Your team operates what we build. We do not create a
dependency and then bill against it.
